# Plane 4 — Skeleton

## Header
Logo/Home; primary navigation; distinct Get Help and Donate actions; equivalent mobile menu.

## Homepage hierarchy
1 Hero: concise value proposition + Get Help + Donate
2 Who We Are
3 Assistance: funeral + insurance
4 How It Works
5 Community/Impact
6 Partners
7 Get Involved: Volunteer/Fundraise/Partner
8 Donate
9 Contact/footer

## Get Help
Direct heading; assistance types; eligibility summary; process; confirmed preparation/documents; request-help CTA; contact alternative; privacy expectation before collecting data.

## Page anatomy
Breadcrumb where useful; one H1; orientation paragraph; scannable H2s; contextual CTA; next-step links; footer.

## Responsive/accessibility
Mobile-first; no hover-only information; usable tap targets; semantic landmarks; keyboard operation; visible focus; meaningful links; form labels/errors; alt strategy; adequate contrast; reduced-motion respect. Target WCAG 2.2 AA practices.
