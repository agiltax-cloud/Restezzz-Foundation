## Strategic reset
The September 28, 2026 founder interview supersedes the earlier funeral-first interpretation for current planning.

### 1. Strategy
Primary user: a young adult preparing to transition out of foster care or recently aged out.

Primary need: practical, human, step-by-step support for independent life.

Current first-party support themes:
- housing/resource navigation
- money/banking basics
- employment/job readiness
- entrepreneurship guidance
- life skills
- six-month transition/goal planning
- ongoing mentorship
- resource referrals

Secondary audiences:
- schools/social workers/youth-serving referral partners
- community members reached through outreach
- volunteers/service providers
- donors/business partners

Community outreach described by the founder includes food, blankets/basic needs and neighborhood outreach.

Historical funeral/insurance assistance remains in the evidence set but is not current top-level IA unless re-confirmed.

### 2. Scope
Core rebuilt capabilities:
- explain transition support
- explain support areas in concrete language
- request support
- refer a young adult
- contact a human
- community outreach/events
- volunteer / provide a service / partner
- donate
- manage content in WordPress/Gutenberg
- privacy/accessibility/legal foundations
