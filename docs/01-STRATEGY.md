# Plane 1 — Strategy

## Objective
Create a trustworthy nonprofit site that helps a person understand Rest Ezzz, determine whether assistance may be relevant, and take a clear next step, while enabling supporters to donate, volunteer, fundraise or partner.

## Evidence-backed context
Historical assets identify relief, process, community, campaigns, partners, fundraising, volunteering and donating. Independent partner evidence describes funeral-expense scholarships and insurance assistance. Public records place the organization in Orange, California and show 2026 community participation.

## Primary audience: person/family seeking assistance
Needs: understand help; determine basic fit without unnecessary disclosure; know what happens next; find human contact; encounter respectful language during grief.
Success: reaches an appropriate help/contact action with expectations understood.

## Secondary audiences
Donor: mission clarity, credible use of funds, secure path, trust signals.
Volunteer/fundraiser: concrete ways to help and inquiry path.
Community partner: program context, partnership model, direct contact.

## UX principles
1. Help before promotion.
2. Plain language before nonprofit jargon.
3. Never make grieving users hunt for process information.
4. Claims require evidence.
5. Ask for minimum necessary personal information.
6. Mobile-first and accessible.
7. Get Help and Donate are distinct intents.

## Candidate measures
Get Help completion, application/contact completion, donation progression, volunteer/partner inquiries, content findability. No sensitive applicant data in analytics.
