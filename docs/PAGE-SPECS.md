## 2. Get Help — `/get-help/`

### Purpose
Give a person seeking assistance one clear starting point without requiring them to understand Rest Ezzz's internal organization.

### H1
**Tell us what you need help with.**

### Intro
Rest Ezzz begins by listening, understanding the immediate need, and identifying the most useful next step.

### Support selector
Four cards:
1. **I'm transitioning from foster care**
2. **My family is facing a loss**
3. **I need community or emergency resources**
4. **I'm referring someone**

Cards 1–3 reveal concise relevant information and point to the appropriate program page. Card 4 goes to Refer Someone.

### How support works
Five steps:
1. Tell us what is happening.
2. We review the request.
3. We talk with you about the next step.
4. We connect support/resources that fit.
5. We stay connected when ongoing guidance is part of the program.

### Request Support form
Placed on this page after the process explanation.

### Privacy message
Rest Ezzz does not request Social Security numbers, financial documents, medical records, immigration documents, or government IDs through the first-contact form.

### Bottom section
Contextual FAQ accordion followed by direct phone/email contact.

Primary CTA throughout: **Request Support**
