# Foster-Transition Research — California

Purpose: provide authoritative background for educational/resource content supporting Rest Ezzz's Transition to Independence program. These sources describe California public programs; Rest Ezzz must not imply it is part of, endorsed by, or a substitute for these government programs.

## California Extended Foster Care
California Department of Social Services states that eligible youth may remain in Extended Foster Care until age 21 and may receive case management, housing/financial support and Independent Living Program services.

Source:
https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/extended-foster-care-ab-12

## Transitional Housing Programs
CDSS describes transitional-housing programs for current/former foster youth as providing housing, life-skills training and guidance to support transition to independent living.

Source:
https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/transitional-housing-programs

## Independent Living Program
CDSS states that the Independent Living Program provides training, services and benefits to assist current and former foster youth with self-sufficiency before and after leaving foster care. California counties design services based on local needs.

Source:
https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/independent-living-program

## Financial wellness / transition planning
CDSS publishes transition-age-youth guidance covering goals and life skills including money management and decision-making.

Source:
https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/financial-wellness

## Safe website use
These sources support statements such as:
- California has existing public programs designed to help eligible current/former foster youth transition to adulthood.
- Housing, life skills, employment/education goals and financial skills are recognized transition needs.
- Rest Ezzz should help young adults understand and connect to existing public resources where appropriate, not duplicate or misrepresent them.

## Do not claim
- that all youth automatically qualify for any specific state/county benefit
- that Rest Ezzz controls public eligibility
- that social workers are prohibited from giving ordinary guidance
- that youth necessarily leave foster care at age 18
- that Rest Ezzz is a licensed housing provider unless confirmed
- that Rest Ezzz is a government or county program
