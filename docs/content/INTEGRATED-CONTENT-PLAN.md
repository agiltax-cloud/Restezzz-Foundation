## Content style
- plain language
- practical next steps
- no guilt
- no savior framing
- no unsupported guarantees
- no negative generalizations about foster care
- preserve dignity and agency

## Operational fact rule
Unverified facts are omitted until confirmed. The page, hierarchy, CTA and interaction model remain fixed.

This applies to:
- eligibility
- geography
- assistance amounts
- insurance details
- sponsor benefits
- legal/tax claims
- payment details
- current contact information
- sensitive stories

## Final page/copy authority
Use:
- `FINAL-WEBSITE-BLUEPRINT.md`
- `PAGE-SPECS.md`
- `FORMS-AND-INTERACTIONS.md`
- `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`

Evidence documents support truth/provenance; they do not re-open UX decisions.
