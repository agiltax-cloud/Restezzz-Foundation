# Rest Ezzz — Integrated Content & IA Plan

## One organization, one integrated direction

The rebuild is not a reproduction of the current live site and not a replacement that erases it.

It combines:
- the restored current website
- the September 28, 2026 founder interview
- current/restored WordPress data and media
- embedded Rest Ezzz Instagram activity
- historical archive material
- verified public/partner sources
- the new Acts of Compassion direction

## Strategic brand umbrella

**Rest Ezzz is an outreach nonprofit that helps people move through difficult transitions with practical support, human connection, resources, and acts of compassion.**

Within that umbrella, the site should organize the work into three primary service/community pillars and one participation ecosystem.

## Mission direction

### Current published mission
"To inspire, nurture, and give Hope to the human spirit by our Rest one family and neighborhood at a time."

### Proposed integrated mission — approval required
**To inspire, nurture, and bring hope to the human spirit by helping one young person, one family, and one neighborhood at a time.**

This preserves the authentic core of the current mission while making room for the founder's current transition-youth direction.

## Vision direction

### Proposed integrated vision — approval required
**A community where every helping hand, every conversation, and every act of compassion creates connection, stability, and hope.**

## Brand language to preserve
High-value authentic language already used by Rest Ezzz:
- Rest on us…
- Uplift the human spirit
- Make a difference
- Helping hands
- We bring comfort in difficult times
- One family and neighborhood at a time
- There is no exercise better for the heart than reaching down and lifting people up

Do not use all of these as slogans at once. They are a language bank for the final brand system.

## Content safety / accuracy rules

1. "Published on the old/current site" is not the same as "currently operational."
2. Do not repeat claims about medical assistance, fee-free fundraising, sponsor benefits, insurance assistance, or financial-aid limits until confirmed.
3. Do not publish sensitive founder history without Lucy's explicit approval.
4. Do not publish beneficiary stories without consent.
5. Do not copy template/demo assets or copy into the new site.
6. Do not use broad claims about foster-care systems without reliable sources.
7. For California foster-transition resource content, ground factual statements in CDSS sources.
8. Verify the public address and IRS/501(c)(3)/EIN language before launch.


## Authoritative research anchors

### California foster-transition context
Use California Department of Social Services as the factual source for public-program context:
- Extended Foster Care: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/extended-foster-care-ab-12
- Transitional Housing Programs: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/transitional-housing-programs
- Independent Living Program: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/independent-living-program
- Financial Wellness: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/foster-care/financial-wellness

These support educational statements about existing California transition resources. They do **not** define Rest Ezzz eligibility and must not be used to imply Rest Ezzz is a government program.

### Current public corroboration
- City of Orange 2026 council minutes document recent Rest Ezzz participation in a nonprofit permit lottery.
- Saturated In His Love publicly lists Rest Ezzz as a partner associated with funeral-expense scholarships and insurance assistance.

## Current website technical facts affecting the rebuild
The restored production site currently uses WordPress + Avada and exposes GiveWP, Contact Form 7, WPForms, Instagram/social-feed and Slider Revolution functionality.

The current homepage SEO description and Open Graph image are still Avada demo content. The rebuild must replace those with Rest Ezzz-specific SEO metadata and imagery.

The new site should preserve data/functionality that matters (especially donation data/forms) without carrying forward unnecessary Avada/demo dependencies.
