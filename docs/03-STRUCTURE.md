## Flows
Assistance: Home/Get Help → overview → service → eligibility/process → request help/contact.
Donor: Home/Impact → substantiated mission/impact → Donate → approved processor.
Volunteer: Get Involved → Volunteer → expectations → inquiry.
Partner: About/Impact → Partners → Partner With Us → inquiry.

Use progressive disclosure for complex assistance information. Do not force donation messaging into the assistance flow. Clearly identify external-service handoffs.
